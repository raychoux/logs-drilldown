The complete Logs Drilldown development stack is installed in Kubernetes.

Verify it:
  make status
  make test

Local endpoints are exposed by Docker Desktop LoadBalancer services:
  Grafana:    http://localhost:3001/grafana
  Loki:       http://localhost:3100
  Tempo:      http://localhost:3200
  Prometheus: http://localhost:9090
  Alloy:      http://localhost:12345

Pod monitor dashboard:
  http://localhost:3001/grafana/d/{{ .Values.grafana.dashboardUid }}/{{ .Values.grafana.dashboardSlug }}

Logs Drilldown service:
  pod-monitor-demo
